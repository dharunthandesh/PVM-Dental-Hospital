Permanent Solution for Missing Teeth in Madurai

Missing teeth can affect your smile, chewing comfort, speech, and jawbone density over time. Dental implants are medical-grade titanium posts placed securely into the jawbone to serve as stable roots for realistic porcelain crowns or bridges. At PVM Dental Clinic in Ponmeni, Madurai, Dr. Mayuri AP evaluates jawbone structure and provides precise, durable dental implant solutions.

Benefits of Biocompatible Dental Implants

  • Natural Look & Feel: Blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth for a confident smile.
  • Long-Term Durability: Fuses directly with jawbone tissue (osseointegration) for lifelong strength.
  • Preserves Jawbone: Stimulates bone growth to prevent facial sagging caused by missing tooth roots.
  • No Damage to Adjacent Teeth: Unlike conventional bridges, neighboring teeth do not require grinding.
  • Restores Full Chewing Power: Eat your favorite foods comfortably without slipping or discomfort.

The Dental Implant Procedure Step-by-Step

1

Diagnostic Evaluation & Planning

Comprehensive oral evaluation and digital X-rays to assess bone volume and plan precise implant placement.

2

Implant Placement

The biocompatible titanium implant is surgically positioned in the jawbone under local anesthesia.

3

Healing & Osseointegration

The bone fuses with the implant post over a few weeks, building a solid foundation.

4

Custom Crown Placement

A custom shade-matched ceramic crown is connected, completing your natural restored tooth.
