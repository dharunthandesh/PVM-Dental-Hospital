How do I choose the right dental hospital in Madurai?

When choosing a dental hospital in Madurai, look for qualified dental practitioners (BDS/M.F.O.), rigorous sterilization protocols, modern equipment, transparent treatment guidance, and round-the-clock emergency support. At PVM Dental Hospital in Ponmeni, Dr. Mayuri AP offers personalized, compassionate dental care in a comfortable setting.

Where can I get root canal treatment (RCT) in Madurai?

PVM Dental Hospital in Ponmeni, Madurai offers modern, gentle root canal therapy (RCT). Using advanced rotary endodontics and digital diagnostics, we gently clean the infected pulp, eliminate toothache, and preserve your natural tooth with custom dental crowns.

What dental treatments does PVM Dental Hospital provide in Madurai?

PVM Dental Hospital provides a full range of dental treatments including painless root canal therapy (RCT), dental implants, clear teeth aligners, orthodontic braces, maxillofacial prosthetics, laser dentistry, tooth-colored restorations, dental scaling/cleaning, and 24-hour emergency care.

Is emergency dental treatment available 24 hours in Madurai?

Yes, PVM Dental Hospital provides 24 hours emergency dental support in Madurai for sudden severe toothaches, chipped or knocked-out teeth, facial swelling, and accidental dental injuries. Call our helpline at +91 96771 04464 or +91 96009 66964 for immediate assistance.

How often should I visit a dental clinic for teeth cleaning?

Dentists recommend professional dental checkups and teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing) every 6 months. Regular cleanings remove stubborn tartar and plaque deposits that regular brushing misses, preventing gum infections and cavities.
