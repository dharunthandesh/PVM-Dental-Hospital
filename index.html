What We Offer

9 Surgeries and Treatments

From diagnostic consultation to complex maxillofacial surgeries — our specialized services are designed to care for your teeth and gums.

Maxillofacial Prosthetics

Reconstructive prostheses for cosmetic and functional rehabilitation of complex jaw, mouth, and facial defects.

RCT (Root Canal)

Painless root canal therapy utilizing state-of-the-art techniques to resolve deep tooth infections and protect natural teeth.

Dental Restoration

High-quality cosmetic fillings, inlays, onlays, dental bridges, and crowns to restore strength, function, and aesthetics.

Oral & Maxillofacial

Specialized surgical interventions for wisdom tooth extractions, jaw reconstructions, facial traumas, and oral pathologies.

Dental Implant Surgery

Permanent, secure replacement of missing teeth with modern biocompatible titanium implants that mimic natural tooth roots.

Laser Dentistry

Advanced dental laser technologies for bloodless, painless gum surgeries, disinfection, and instant smile bleaching.

Dental Consultation

Detailed oral examinations, comprehensive health assessments, diagnostic checkups, and tailored treatment plans.

24 Hours Dentists

Dedicated emergency dental team standing by 24/7 to treat severe toothaches, facial swellings, or accidental dental injuries.

Dentistry

Routine care including scaling, polishing, deep tooth cleanings, cavities prevention, and patient health advice.

Teeth Aligners

Removable, transparent, custom-built clear aligners to straighten crooked teeth without the look of metal braces.

Dental Braces

Conventional metallic, ceramic, and self-ligating orthodontic braces to systematically resolve bite and alignment issues.
