Overview of Root Canal Therapy in Madurai

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) is a routine dental procedure designed to remove infected or inflamed pulp tissue from inside a tooth. When deep tooth decay, cracks, or trauma reach the inner nerve space, it can cause severe tooth pain and swelling. At PVM Dental Clinic in Ponmeni, Madurai, Dr. Mayuri AP uses advanced rotary endodontics and local anesthetics to ensure a completely comfortable, painless experience.

Signs You May Need a Root Canal

Common symptoms that indicate pulp infection or tooth decay requiring endodontic evaluation include:

  • Persistent severe toothache while chewing or biting
  • Heightened sensitivity to hot or cold food and drinks
  • Swelling or tenderness in the gums near the affected tooth
  • Discoloration or darkening of the damaged tooth
  • Recurring pimple-like bump (abscess) on the gum line

Step-by-Step Root Canal Procedure at PVM Dental Clinic

1

Clinical Examination & X-Ray Diagnostics

Detailed digital assessment to determine root anatomy and extent of infection.

2

Gentle Anesthesia & Canal Cleaning

Disinfection & Sealing

4

Crown Restoration

A custom ceramic or zirconia crown is fitted over the tooth to restore original strength and natural appearance.
