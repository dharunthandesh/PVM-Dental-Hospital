Safe & Painless Dental Extractions in Madurai

While our primary goal is always to save natural teeth whenever possible, a tooth extraction may become necessary when a tooth is severely decayed, fractured beyond repair, or impacted. At PVM Dental Clinic in Ponmeni, Madurai, Dr. Mayuri AP ensures complete local anesthesia so patients experience zero pain during surgical procedures.

When is Tooth Extraction Necessary?

  • Severe tooth decay extending deep beneath the gum line
  • Traumatic tooth fracture that cannot support a crown
  • Impacted wisdom teeth causing jaw pain, infection, or overcrowding
  • Advanced gum disease (periodontitis) with loose teeth
  • Orthodontic tooth removal to create space for braces alignment

Impacted Wisdom Tooth Surgical Removal

Wisdom teeth (third molars) often lack sufficient space to erupt properly, becoming trapped inside the jawbone or emerging at painful angles. Dr. Mayuri AP performs minor oral surgical extractions to gently remove impacted wisdom teeth, relieving pressure and protecting adjacent teeth from damage.

Post-Extraction Care & Quick Healing Guidance

1

Gauzepad Pressure

Bite firmly on sterile gauze for 30–45 minutes following extraction to promote clotting.

2

Cold Compress

Apply an ice pack externally against the cheek for 10–15 minutes to prevent swelling.

3

Soft Food & Warm Salt Rinse

Consume cool, soft foods for the first 24 hours. Gentle warm saltwater mouth rinses starting the next day.
